06 · The declared stay

Hotel · district · address · duration

The declared stay can support service, safety and planning.

Use the hotel or address only for approved services, emergency communication and aggregate tourism planning.

For the visitorConfidence

Confirm the intended hotel or address and receive relevant arrival guidance.

For public serviceReadiness

Define lawful communication cohorts during a security, health or weather event.

For tourism policyVisibility

See aggregate destination and overnight-demand patterns earlier.

Critical distinctionA declared stay is not continuous location tracking.

Evidence and design implication

  • Bangladesh Immigration E-Arrival Card already includes address type, hotel, district, thana, detailed address and stay duration.
  • New Zealand Traveller Declaration: privacy and purpose model.

The proposed service uses the declared accommodation only for approved purposes and periods; it does not imply live tracking.

Proposed commercial model

This is a Tourifye recommendation, not an existing government policy. It separates the statutory declaration from optional commercial discovery. Advertising would be clearly labelled, policy-approved and unable to influence border decisions. Personal arrival data would not be sold.
